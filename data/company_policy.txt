Company Work Policy

Employees can work from home two days per week.

The standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM.

Employees receive 24 paid leaves every year.

New employees have a probation period of six months.

Travel reimbursement claims must be submitted within 15 days.

Employees must inform their manager before taking planned leave.

The company provides health insurance to all full-time employees.

Employees are eligible for performance reviews every six months.
